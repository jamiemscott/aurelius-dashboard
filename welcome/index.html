Why it matters

Cash feels safe. It rarely is.

Holding cash feels like the cautious choice — nothing to lose, nothing at risk. But prices rise every year, and money that isn't growing is quietly losing ground against them. The risk isn't always the one you can see.

Time, not timing

The best time to start was years ago.
The second best is today.

Growth compounds — returns earn their own returns, year after year. That means the single biggest factor in most people's outcome isn't picking the perfect moment to invest, it's simply the number of years their money has had to work. Waiting is the expensive choice, even when it doesn't feel like one.
