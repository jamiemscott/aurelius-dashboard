Style Guide

Onboarding CTA

The sidebar onboarding prompt card — a gold-accented panel that nudges new users to complete their profile, verify identity, or explore features. Defined in modules/ob-cta.css. Hides entirely at ≤900px (icon rail) where there is no space.

Anatomy (.ob-cta)

The card renders inside the sidebar below the nav and adviser card. A dismiss button (.ob-cta__dismiss) is absolutely positioned in the top-right corner, requiring padding-inline-end: 18px on .ob-cta__top for clearance. The CTA button uses .ob-cta__btn modifier — reduced size, full width.

.ob-cta__btn Modifier

Like .contact-btn, this is a size-only modifier applied on top of a base button class. Sets font-size: var(--fs-md) (12px) and padding: 7px 12px. Width 100% to fill the narrow sidebar card. Always combine with .btn-primary or .btn-secondary.

Responsive Behaviour

The card adapts at two breakpoints before disappearing entirely:

Breakpoint Behaviour
>1280pxFull card with eyebrow, title, sub, and CTA button
≤1280pxEyebrow and sub hidden; title smaller; CTA remains
≤900pxEntire .ob-cta hidden with display: none (icon rail — no sidebar text)

Do & Don't

Do

Show the CTA only when there is a specific onboarding action the user hasn't yet completed. Once dismissed or completed, remove it from the sidebar.

Don't

Don't stack multiple .ob-cta cards — the sidebar has limited height. If multiple prompts are needed, use a single rotating card or a progress-step bar instead.

Accessibility Notes

The dismiss button must have a descriptive aria-label — "Dismiss this prompt" rather than just "Dismiss". The card is a <div> (not a live region) unless the content changes dynamically, in which case use aria-live="polite". The CTA button's purpose must be clear from its text label alone — avoid generic labels like "Click here".
