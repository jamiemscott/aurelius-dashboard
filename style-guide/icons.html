Style Guide

Icons

Every interface icon used across the customer site and the adviser dashboard: 77 icons on a 24 × 24 grid, drawn as strokes in currentColor so they take the colour of the text around them. Copy the SVG to use one inline, or download it as an SVG or a PNG in any of the brand colours. App and product icons are on Resources.

Colour, size and download

Pick a colour and size: the previews and every PNG link below follow it, and the zip holds the whole set. SVGs are always currentColor. Stroke widths are as used on the site (1.7 in navigation, 2 to 2.5 in buttons); change stroke-width to adjust.
