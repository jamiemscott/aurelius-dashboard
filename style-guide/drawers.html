Style Guide

Drawers

Panels that slide in from the right edge. Every drawer in the app is a .drawer: adviser messages, notifications, add fund and add investment. They share one shell, header, notice, body and footer, so only the content changes. Defined in modules/drawers.css.

Anatomy

A header with media, a title and a subtitle on the left and actions on the right. An optional notice strip. A body, the only part that scrolls. An optional footer that stays in view. Where the browser supports scroll-driven animations, the bottom of the body fades while there is more to see, and the fade goes once you reach the end.

Animation & Transition

Closed, a drawer sits off the right edge at translateX(100%). :popover-open, or .is-open on OutSystems, brings it to translateX(0) with a decelerating curve (cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)) over 0.36s. @starting-style gives the popover a first frame to animate from.

The backdrop fades and blurs in over the same time. allow-discrete on display and overlay keeps the drawer on screen while it slides out. With reduced motion turned on, drawers open and close without sliding.

On OutSystems

ODC doesn't use the Popover API, so there is no ::backdrop. Toggle is-open on the drawer, and show a .drawer-overlay next to it for the blurred backdrop. Put both at the end of the screen, outside the layout, so nothing clips them.

<div class="drawer is-open" role="dialog" aria-labelledby="adviser-drawer-title">
  <!-- header, notice, body, footer as above -->
</div>
<div class="drawer-overlay"></div>

<!-- To close: remove is-open from the drawer and hide the overlay -->

Accessibility Notes

Each drawer is a role="dialog" named by its title through aria-labelledby. The title is an h2, so it appears in the heading list too. The media is decorative and hidden from screen readers.

A popover isn't modal, so don't add aria-modal. The page behind stays reachable, and Escape or a click outside closes the drawer. Opened from its trigger, the drawer comes next in the tab order, and focus goes back to the trigger when it closes. On OutSystems, move focus into the drawer when it opens, return it to the trigger when it closes, and close on Escape.

The close button is labelled "Close". Text actions like "Mark all read" use their visible words as their name, so speech users can say what they see.
