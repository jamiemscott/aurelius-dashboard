Where files live

Brand files go in src/images/brand/, and a new product gets its own folder under icons/. File names are lowercase with hyphens, with no spaces. Add new assets to this page too.

src/images/
├── brand/
│   ├── aurelius-logo-variations.png
│   ├── aurelius-monogram.svg          theme colour, source for --brand-monogram
│   ├── aurelius-monogram.eps
│   ├── aurelius-monogram-amber.svg
│   ├── aurelius-monogram-amber.png
│   └── icons/
│       ├── app/                       one folder per product
│       ├── library/
│       ├── library-beta/
│       └── style-guide/
├── wave.svg
